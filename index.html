Frequently asked questions

everything you need to know before your first self-drive rental.

Do I need to pay any security deposit? +

No, We do not take any kind of security deposit.

How can I pay the rent?+

We accept all credit and debit cards. Upload your card details on our secure platform and we will handle the rest.

Who will deliver the car? +

One of the DriveX partners closest to your location will deliver the car to you.

Who can rent a car?+

The minimum age for hiring a car is 18 years, and you should have a valid CNIC and driving licence. If you are on a tourist visa, you need a valid home-country licence, an international driving licence and a valid passport with a visa stamp.

Are the cars insured? +

Yes, all cars come with comprehensive insurance as per Pakistani law. In case of any accident or damage, a police report is mandatory by law. Costs over and above the insured amount are your responsibility.

Do I need to pay a surcharge in case of a traffic fine? +

Yes, there will be a surcharge.
